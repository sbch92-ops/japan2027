Group tour includes
Guide for ~3h, special entrance, lounge with a drink, guided rides in Super Nintendo World (Mario Kart, Mine-Cart Madness, Yoshi's Adventure) plus JAWS, 4 Express rides of your choice, 10% off meals, certificate and poncho.
Private tour includes
Your own guide and custom plan, VIP entrance and lounge, priority on rides incl. Super Nintendo World, shows, meal(s) at a tour-only restaurant, luggage storage, one free parking space.
Party size
Group tour: per person, up to ~10 strangers per group. Private: priced as a party of 4 even if you are 2; up to 10 people, extra guests from ~¥62,000 each.
Ages
Children 3 and under free; under-12s must be with someone 13 or older; normal ride height and age limits apply, with no swaps.
Park ticket
Not included. Buy a Studio Pass (¥8,600–11,000 pp, RM222–284) for the same date.
How to book
Official USJ online ticket store (usjticketing.com) or resellers like KKday/Klook. English-guide tours need booking at least 1 month ahead. No changes or refunds once bought. Weekend and holiday slots sell out first.
